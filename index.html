About the Marketplace

A single place to compare verified pharmacies on price, rating and delivery speed.

Many pharmacies, one catalog

The same medicine is listed by several verified pharmacies. You see every seller's price, stock and delivery time side by side, then choose.

Prescription-aware

Schedule H and H1 medicines require a valid prescription. Those orders are held until a registered pharmacist reviews and verifies the upload.

Tracked end to end

Every order moves through six connected stages — Placed, Accepted, Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery and Delivered — visible to you and the seller.

How It Works

From search to doorstep in four steps.

  1. 1

    Search & compare

    Find a medicine by brand or generic name and compare every verified pharmacy selling it.

  2. 2

    Add a prescription

    For prescription-only medicines, upload your doctor's prescription during checkout.

  3. 3

    Pharmacist verifies

    A registered pharmacist at the selling pharmacy reviews the prescription before packing.

  4. 4

    Track to delivery

    Follow your order through all six fulfilment stages until it reaches your door.

Marketplace Trust, Safety & Quality Assurance

Our 3-pillar safety protocol covers genuine medicines, verified partner pharmacies, and patient protection.

100% Genuine Medicines

Sourced from Licensed Chemists: Partnered strictly with CDSCO-registered local pharmacies.
Zero Counterfeit Policy: Mandatory batch number & expiry date verification on every item.
Tamper-Proof Sealed Packaging: Hygienic, private, and tamper-evident packaging.
Cold-Chain Maintenance: Insulated thermal packing for insulins, biologics, and vaccines.

Rx & Patient Safety First

Registered Pharmacist Review: All Schedule H/H1 prescriptions verified before packing.
Dosage & Quantity Checks: Safety thresholds preventing excessive dosage orders.
Confidential Health Data: Patient profiles & prescription files handled confidentially.
Transparent Review Guidance: Immediate notification if a prescription needs updating.

Customer Protection & Support

Multi-Pharmacy Price Transparency: Compare package pricing & ratings across sellers.
Refund Support: Refund claim workflow for damaged, wrong, or expired items.
Live 6-Stage Tracking: Connected order progression from placement to delivery.
Patient Care Desk: Support for orders, prescriptions, and product advice.
